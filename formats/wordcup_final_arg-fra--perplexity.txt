### Notes on the Format

- **Header**: `= Competition – Stage` with metadata lines starting with `#`.
- **Match line**: `Date Time Home Score (a.e.t.) (pen) Away @ Venue`
- **Goals**: In `[]` with minute and method (e.g., `(pen)`). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIFA_World_Cup_final)
- **Line-ups**: Position, number, name, captain `(c)`, and in-line subs with `[minute → player]`. [transfermarkt](https://www.transfermarkt.com/argentina_france/index/spielbericht/3975879)
- **Substitutions**: Detailed list with minute, player off/on, position, and cards if applicable.
- **Cards**: Separate section with minute, player, and reason.
- **Penalties**: Paired format showing each taker and outcome for clarity.
- **Extra notes**: Man of the Match, special rules (e.g., concussion sub). [transfermarkt](https://www.transfermarkt.com/argentina_france/index/spielbericht/3975879)

This keeps the file human-readable while being structured enough for parsing.

## Why This Is Compact Yet Parseable

- **Pipe delimiters (`|`)** separate fields cleanly.
- **Key-value pairs** in comments (`# key:value`) for metadata.
- **Consistent patterns**:
  - Match line: `DATE TIME | HOME SCORE AWAY | @:VENUE`
  - Goals: `PLAYER MIN(p); PLAYER MIN`
  - Line-ups: `POS|NUM|NAME → MIN|SUBNAME[tags]`
  - Cards: `TEAM|YMIN|PLAYER|reason`
  - Penalties: `TEAM|ORDER|PLAYER|outcome`
- **Shorthand tags**:
  - `(c)` = captain
  - `[Ymin]` = yellow card at minute
  - `(p)` = penalty goal
  - `[conc]` = concussion substitution

A simple regex or split-on-`|` parser can extract:
- Teams, score, venue, date/time
- Full line-ups with positions, numbers, subs, and cards
- All cards with reasons
- Penalty shoot-out order and outcomes
